> *Kaun is ghar ki dekh-bhaal kare* 

> *Roz ek cheez toot jaati hai.*


---
### Translation
Who is there to look after this house? 

Every day, something breaks.

---


The emptiness of a house without you is not silence. 
It is a noise that only I can hear.
